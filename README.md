# Hollow Creek

A quiet first-person voxel game set in a secluded Appalachian valley.
Unity 6.3 LTS · URP · Smooth Voxel · C#

---

## About

You are a son returning to your deceased father's old cabin. There are no quests, no combat, and no hunger. The valley is yours to wander, to dig, to reshape, and to understand.

The cabin still has warm light in the window. A dirt path leads into the forest and up the ridge to a half-collapsed 1950s fire watchtower. On top of the tower, a rusted logbook, a pair of binoculars, and a hand-drawn map wait with three marked spots.

The father watched this valley from the tower. The son came back to understand why.

---

## Vision

Hollow Creek is not a survival game. It is not a horror game. It is not a voxel sandbox.

It is a quiet story about loss, memory, and letting go — told through a valley, a cabin, and the things a father left behind.

The player decides how to grieve. Stay in the cabin. Move into the cave. Sell the land. Keep it. Disappear into the woods.

Every path leads to a different ending.

---

## Features

- Smooth voxel terrain (Marching Cubes / Surface Nets)
- Chunk streaming with LOD
- Terraforming: dig, build, reshape the land
- Appalachian autumn: volumetric fog, muted greens, reds and oranges
- Day cycle and seasonal atmosphere
- Procedural audio: wind, river, birds, distant sounds
- Backpack inventory with weight system
- Story items: logbook, binoculars, photograph, hand-drawn map
- Hand-built landmarks: cabin, campfire, watchtower, fallen tree ramp
- Custom shaders for trees, terrain, and the shroud
- Multiple endings depending on how the player lives
- Multiplayer (2 players, cooperative) — planned

## Tech Stack

- **Engine:** Unity 6.3 LTS
- **Pipeline:** Universal Render Pipeline (URP)
- **Language:** C#
- **Voxel:** Marching Cubes / Surface Nets, chunked, LOD
- **Shaders:** Custom HLSL for terrain, foliage, fog
- **Multiplayer:** Unity Netcode for GameObjects (later)
- **Target:** PC, 60+ FPS on RTX 3060
